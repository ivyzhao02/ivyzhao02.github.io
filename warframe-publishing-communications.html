The publishing need

Publishing around a live game.

Announcements, events, collaborations, and updates each brought different deadlines, assets, and review needs. I wrote and edited player-facing copy, adapted it for web, social, and in-product channels, and coordinated revisions and approvals with marketing, design, development, and global teams.

My contribution

Getting each piece ready.

I captured and edited gameplay footage, prepared visual assets, and followed up on the details needed for review and publication. I also supported TennoCon 2025 content production and event plans, including moderation of sensitive player discussions.

A practical improvement

A tracker the team could try alongside Jira.

I proposed and built an Excel tracker to give the remote team a quick view of assignments, edit readiness, and approval status. The team tried it during my internship. It focused on a particular visibility gap alongside Jira.

What I took from the role

Writing is one part of publishing.

Publishing for a live game connects writing with timing, assets, reviews, and audience context. Preparing the message and keeping those details moving were both part of my contribution.

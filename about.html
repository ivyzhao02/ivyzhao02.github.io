About

A little more about me.

I like understanding both the audience and the work behind the message: what people need to know, which details matter, and how to keep everyone aligned.

Working perspective

Professional background

I’ve worked across live-service game publishing, travel insurance, public-sector HR communications, and financial services. The industries differ, but the pattern is similar: writing clearly, tracking details, coordinating stakeholders, and helping work reach its audience.

I graduated from the University of Waterloo with a BA in Communication Studies through the Honours Arts & Business co-op program. That mix of communication, business context, and hands-on experience shaped how I approach my role today: learn carefully, understand what each person needs, record the important decisions, and follow issues through.

Games have remained a consistent part of both my professional experience and community life. I’m especially interested in games publishing, PR and communications, content and community work, and production coordination roles where audience awareness and dependable delivery meet.

Working with me

Curious, prepared, and comfortable in the middle of things.

What I gravitate toward

Where a good message needs a dependable path.

I enjoy the point where audience needs, creative ideas, and practical delivery meet. That might mean shaping a player-facing update, making a live conversation easier to run, or helping a team see what still needs a decision.

What colleagues can expect

Useful questions and honest follow-through.

I ask for context early, write down decisions, share concerns before they become surprises, and stay calm when plans change. I care about making it easier for other people to do their part well.

A small personal note

Outside my day-to-day role.

I volunteer with BlightFall as Community & Operations Lead. I stay close to what players are asking for across a community of approximately 2,000 members, guide community and brand communication, and help shape the project infrastructure behind decisions, reporting, coordination, and follow-through.

The work gives me room to think about how people find information, participate in online spaces, and keep a distributed project moving without losing context. I also maintain creative web projects of my own, including this evolving record of my experience and professional direction.

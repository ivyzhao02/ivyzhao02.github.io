About

A little more about me.

I like understanding both the audience and the work behind the message: what people need to know, which details matter, and how to keep everyone aligned.

Working perspective

I’ve worked across live-service game publishing, travel insurance, public-sector HR communications, and financial services. The industries differ, but the pattern is similar: writing clearly, tracking details, coordinating stakeholders, and helping work reach its audience.

I graduated from the University of Waterloo with a BA in Communication Studies through the Honours Arts & Business co-op program. That mix of communication, business context, and hands-on experience shaped how I approach my role today: learn carefully, understand what each person needs, record the important decisions, and follow issues through.

Games have remained a consistent part of both my professional experience and community life. I’m especially interested in games publishing, PR and communications, content and community work, and production coordination roles where audience awareness and dependable delivery meet.

Working principles

A few habits I rely on.

01

Write for the person receiving it

Good communication starts with what the audience already knows, what they need next, and which details deserve emphasis.

02

Make ownership visible

Shared status, owners, and next steps help creative and operational partners coordinate without chasing updates.

03

Prepare, then adapt

I prepare carefully for presentations, training, and live questions, then adjust based on what the room needs.

04

Follow through

I stay close to feedback, open questions, and practical details until the next step is understood.

A small personal note

Outside my day-to-day role.

I volunteer as Community Management Lead for an independent game project, where I oversee Discord systems, moderation, announcements, events, member support, and feedback between players and developers. I also maintain creative web projects of my own.

Both give me room to think about how people find information, participate in online spaces, and build trust through consistent communication. This site is one of those projects: an evolving record of my experience and professional direction.

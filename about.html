About

A little more about me.

I’m passionate about games, the industry, and the communities around them. I love being part of projects that bring those interests together, whether I’m helping a team communicate with players or supporting the work behind the scenes.

My background

Games, communications, and the day-to-day details.

My background spans Warframe publishing at Digital Extremes, HR communications, financial services, and claims payments. I graduated from the University of Waterloo with a BA in Communication Studies through the Honours Arts & Business co-op program.

I’m interested in games publishing, PR and communications, community work, and production coordination. I enjoy the mix of writing, audience understanding, and practical planning that helps an idea reach people.

The parts I enjoy

Finding the question that needs an answer.

I enjoy writing something people can use, preparing for a live conversation, and figuring out which question needs an answer before a team can move on. BlightFall brings those interests together through community listening, events, and the planning behind the project.

How I work

Ask, prepare, and follow through.

I like to understand the context before getting started, keep a record of decisions, and ask questions while there’s time to act on the answers. Preparation helps me adapt when the conversation or the plan changes.

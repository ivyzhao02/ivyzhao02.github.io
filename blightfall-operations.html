BlightFall / Community & Operations Lead

BlightFall Operations & Product Infrastructure.

I planned a staged move from Notion to a shared system for BlightFall’s decisions, tasks, and project history. I designed how that information would be organized, directed implementation, and reviewed and tested the results with the team.

Migration completed in August 2026.

The practical need

Why change the workspace?

The team needed a way to keep its planning and project history available without depending on a Notion subscription. I reviewed the existing workspace, identified what needed to be kept, and organized the move into smaller stages. That review covered 168 exported entries.

A migration decision

Check each stage before relying on it.

I planned each stage so the team could check the new version before relying on it. I defined what to preserve, what a successful transfer would look like, and how to recover if a change went wrong. The migration was accepted after testing and an observation period, and the old workspace was retired in August 2026.

Information design

Giving project information a home.

I designed a shared home for decisions, actions, risks, and meeting notes, with owners and next steps attached. I also specified how it would connect with the team’s development tracker and reference documents.

My contribution

Planning, implementation direction, and review.

I planned the migration, designed the information layout, and wrote the requirements and review criteria. I directed implementation with AI tools, reviewed the outputs, and coordinated testing and feedback with the team.

I also specified checks so changes could be reviewed before they were saved, conflicting updates could be caught, and a history of changes would remain available.

Reporting

Making the information useful to different people.

I specified and tested ways to turn the same project records into useful updates for leadership and the team. These include readable previews, downloadable reports, short Discord summaries, and weekly digests.

Testing and acceptance

Checking the move before relying on it.

I coordinated checks of meeting workflows, how information moved between tools, and whether reports matched their source records. I also checked backup copies and brought the team into the review before each transition.

The result

Decisions, ownership, and updates in one place.

The completed migration gave the team a consistent way to record decisions, find ownership and next steps, and prepare updates from the same project information. Alongside the workspace, I established the project’s domain, business email foundation, and social presence.

More about the implementation

The workspace stores structured records in a private repository. Automated checks support record quality, reviewable updates, and recovery. Reporting can produce Markdown and PDF files as well as shorter summaries.

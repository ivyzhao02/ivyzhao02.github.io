How I approach communication.

01 / Audience

Write for the person receiving it

I think about what the audience needs to know, how much context they have, and what should be easiest to understand first.

02 / Collaboration

Keep context moving

I track feedback, approvals, and open questions so creative and operational partners can make informed decisions.

03 / Presence

Prepare, then adapt

Whether I’m supporting a presentation, leading training, or answering questions live, I prepare carefully and adjust to the room.

04 / Follow-through

Stay with the details

I record what matters, involve the right people, and follow an issue through until the next step is understood.

Leading a growing pre-launch game community.

Situation and role

Building the community layer before launch.

My role is to help create a community space that players can understand, moderators can run, and developers can learn from.

I joined the volunteer team on June 17, 2026. As membership increased, I took responsibility for the day-to-day community environment while working directly with lead developers on announcements, feedback, events, and server needs.

Community systems

Channels, roles, permissions, and guardrails.

I manage the server structure that shapes how members enter, find information, ask for help, and participate. That includes channel organization, roles and permissions, bots, AutoMod, announcements, moderation workflows, escalation handling, events, and member support.

The goal is not to add complexity for its own sake. I adjust the server as needs emerge so expectations remain understandable, staff have useful controls, and members can participate without unnecessary friction.

Growth period

Keeping the space organized as membership increased.

Available Server Insights show membership rising from 484 on June 26 to 1,051 by the final exported day, July 27. The confirmed community count reached 1,125 on July 29.

I do not treat that growth as an individual acquisition result. My contribution was leading the community operations around it: maintaining the server, supporting members, handling moderation needs, and helping the team adapt as the audience became larger and more active.

Live developer Stage

Moderating a live build-and-feedback session.

I planned and moderated a roughly 2.5-hour Discord Stage with approximately 40 peak attendees. Developers demonstrated Roblox Studio work, developed live, answered player questions, and gathered feedback from the community.

During the event, I configured and managed speakers, highlighted questions that had been missed, monitored the accompanying chat, and relayed useful player feedback. When external GIFs became disruptive, I temporarily restricted stickers and embeds so the conversation could continue without overcorrecting the wider event.

The session required preparation, calm live judgment, and the ability to support both the presenters and the audience at the same time.

Developer collaboration

Turning community activity into useful context.

I collaborate with lead developers on community needs, member feedback, announcements, planned activities, and server systems. That means identifying what needs attention, separating isolated reactions from recurring themes, and making sure relevant player context reaches the people who can act on it.

I also translate development information back into community-facing communication, helping members understand what is happening without overstating what has been decided.

Earlier communities

Two Warframe clan Discords.

From approximately 2019 to 2021, I founded and configured two Warframe clan communities from scratch. Each grew to approximately 300-400 members as part of a larger clan alliance community of roughly 1,600 members at peak.

I built channel, role, and permission structures; configured bots; moderated member issues; supported activities and events; and eventually transferred Discord and in-game leadership to successor owners through planned handoffs.

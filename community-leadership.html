Volunteer experience / June 2026 - Present

Leading the BlightFall community.

I joined BlightFall in June 2026 and volunteer as Community & Operations Lead. My community work includes Discord administration, moderation, onboarding, player support, announcements, feedback, and live developer events for the Roblox game project.

The community need

Making a growing server easier to use.

Players need places to find information, ask questions, and share ideas. Staff need practical guidance for responding. I manage channels, permissions, bots, AutoMod, and member support, and work with lead developers on community needs and announcements.

Listening and follow-through

From player suggestions to developer conversations.

Dozens of suggestions can arrive in a day. I look for recurring themes, answer questions, update the FAQ, and relay useful context to developers. Once the team has decided what to share, I help communicate it back to players.

A decision in practice

Keeping a live Stage on track.

I planned and moderated two Discord Stages where developers demonstrated Roblox Studio work, built live, answered questions, and gathered feedback. I managed speakers, surfaced missed questions, monitored chat, and supported the conversation between presenters and players.

During the first Stage, GIF spam began interrupting the discussion. I temporarily restricted external stickers and embeds so the session could continue. The first event ran for roughly 2.5 hours with about 40 peak attendees; the second ran for about three hours with about 70.

Staff and onboarding

Supporting staff and new members.

As the staff team grew, I clarified responsibilities, refined elevated permissions, updated rules and guidance, and reorganized moderation logs. I also set up Rules Screening, the Server Guide, Resource Pages, FAQ and link references, moderation 2FA, and the server’s Discovery presence.

Brand and participation

Inviting players into the project.

I designed BlightFall’s logo and helped shape its wording and brand direction outside the game. A server-wide poll about the brand colour prompted active discussion about the project’s identity, giving players a way to participate and the team a chance to hear how they understood it.

Earlier experience

Two Warframe clan communities.

From approximately 2019 to 2021, I founded two Warframe clan Discords, each of which grew to around 300–400 members within a larger alliance community of roughly 1,600 at its peak. I configured the servers, ran activities, moderated, and handed ownership to successors by January 2021.
